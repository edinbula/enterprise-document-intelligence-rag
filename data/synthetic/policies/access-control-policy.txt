SYNTHETIC ACCESS CONTROL POLICY

Document ID: POL-SEC-001
Version: 2.1
Owner: Information Security
Classification: Internal
Effective date: 2026-01-15
Review date: 2027-01-15

1. Purpose

This policy defines access-control requirements for the fictional Northstar
Financial Services organization. It is synthetic demonstration content and
does not describe a real company or production environment.

2. Access principles

Access to information systems must follow least-privilege and need-to-know
principles. Each user must receive only the permissions required for their
approved responsibilities.

3. Account management

Every account must be assigned to one identifiable person or approved service.
Shared interactive accounts are prohibited. Service accounts must have a
documented owner, purpose, and review date.

4. Authentication

Administrative accounts must use multi-factor authentication. Passwords and
other credentials must not be stored in source code, documentation, tickets,
or unencrypted configuration files.

5. Authorization reviews

Managers and system owners must review privileged access every 90 days.
Access that is no longer justified must be removed within one business day.

6. Termination and role changes

Access for terminated personnel must be disabled immediately after the
authorized termination event. Access affected by a role change must be
reviewed within one business day.

7. Audit evidence

Authentication events, authorization changes, and privileged operations must
produce traceable audit records. Audit records must include the actor, action,
target, timestamp, and outcome.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions require documented approval from Information Security, a business
owner, an expiration date, and compensating controls.
